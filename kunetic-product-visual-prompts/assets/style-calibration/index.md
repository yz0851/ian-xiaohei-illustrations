# Style Calibration Index

> All images in this folder were visually reviewed on 2026-08-03.
> They are visual calibration references only. They may guide composition, hierarchy, camera distance, scene treatment, typography and annotation style.
> They must not be treated as evidence of KUNETIC product appearance, specifications, certifications, customer projects, factory capability, internal layout or technical functions.
> Before using a reference, open the actual image. Do not claim to have reviewed an image from its filename alone.

## Category use

- `product-hero/`: square Hero carousel references, including product overview, parameters, dimensions and Hero-level applications.
- `product-poster/`: product-led posters where overall visual attraction is more important than a neutral product view.
- `real-application/`: independent application-section visuals and multi-scene application panoramas.
- `customer-real-scene/`: human scale and casual customer-site photography references.
- `topology/`: system nodes, energy-flow arrows and equipment relationship layouts.
- `factory-oem/`: product-family, workshop, assembly and OEM/ODM presentation references.
- `feature-visualization/`: technical features, integration, component callouts and abstract capability expression.
- `deployment-scale/`: dimensions, weight, installation footprint and product-scale presentation.
- `typography-layout/`: parameter hierarchy, labels, title placement and technical typography.

`Primary` references are the clearest examples for their category. `Secondary` references are useful for a narrower pattern. `Caution` references contain visually useful ideas but carry a higher risk of unsupported certification, factory, project or technical claims.
